Open-Air Ceremony

Experience your open-air ceremony in the heart of nature, where your love shines through in every detail. Our gallery shows striking images of lovingly decorated wedding arches, artfully arranged seating beneath tall, venerable trees, and atmospheric details that turn your ceremony into a fairytale experience. Every eye-catcher – from softly glowing lights to delicate floral arrangements – was chosen with the greatest care to underline the magic of your special moment.

Marquee

Get a glimpse of our spacious marquee – a place that’s perfect for stylish dinners and exuberant celebrations. The lovingly decorated space captivates with transparent walls that offer an unobstructed view of the lush greenery, creating a feeling of freedom and openness. Here, modern design meets natural beauty, turning every celebration into an extraordinary experience.
