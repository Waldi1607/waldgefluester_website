Discover the magic of winter at Waldgeflüster! While others hibernate, this is when things get truly cozy here. In the heart of nature, surrounded by snow-covered trees and twinkling lights, you’ll experience Christmas parties, birthdays, and winter events that are guaranteed to stay unforgettable. Planning a company Christmas party or a celebration with friends? With us, your winter event in the forest becomes one of a kind: mulled wine, Bavarian curling & wintry ambiance included.

The Venue – Celebrations in the Heart of the Forest

In winter, Waldgeflüster transforms into a truly special winter and Christmas venue on the Swabian Alb. Amid twinkling lights, a crackling fire, and a snow-covered forest backdrop, an atmosphere emerges that warms the heart and soul.

  • Heated indoor area with bar, dance floor, light & sound system
  • Room for up to 40 guests (seated)
  • Bavarian curling lane, fire pit with lounge seating
  • 2,000 m² of outdoor space with winter lighting & forest backdrop

Bavarian Curling – Fun & Action

A highlight of every winter celebration: our Bavarian curling is the perfect combination of entertainment and friendly athletic fun. Whether you’re seasoned players or complete beginners – the fun always comes first. Organize tournaments, cheer on your colleagues and friends, or simply enjoy the relaxed mood this wintry activity brings with it. It’s a part of the program that’s guaranteed to deliver plenty of laughs and exciting moments.

Mulled Wine Reception – the Perfect Start to the Evening

Start your winter event with a mulled wine reception in the middle of an enchanting winter setting. At Waldgeflüster, every Christmas party begins with a smile and a warming cup in hand. Our team welcomes your guests with hot drinks like mulled wine, punch, or hot chocolate, while you let yourselves be enchanted by sparkling string lights, atmospheric lighting, and wintry decorations. The crisp winter air and the cozy atmosphere set the stage for the perfect start to your celebration.

Festive Dinner – Winter Cuisine with Heart

After an atmospheric reception and a round of Bavarian curling, we invite you to a festive dinner. Together with our experienced catering partners, we serve you regional winter menus, festive buffets, and Christmas specialties – your culinary enjoyment takes center stage. Pair your dinner with our beverage service, ranging from fine wines to wintry cocktails. The focus is on quality, regional sourcing, and atmosphere – turning your evening into a delight for all the senses.

Campfire & Cozy Togetherness

The fire pit blazes, the lights twinkle, conversations grow softer, the laughter lingers. Here you can let the evening wind down in peace – with mulled wine, music, and a feeling of togetherness. Cozy blankets, marshmallows for roasting, and a selection of hot drinks create a warm and truly special atmosphere. The campfire at Waldgeflüster is the perfect place for unforgettable moments and the closing of your Christmas party.

Why Waldgeflüster for Your Winter Event?

  • A Winter Atmosphere Close to Nature

    Our venue lies in the heart of nature and transforms into a true winter fairytale. Snow, frosty air, and the stillness of the forest make your event something truly special. Every moment here feels authentic and magical.

  • Flexibility & Individuality

    Whether you’re planning a small, intimate celebration or a larger corporate event – we shape your winter event around your ideas. Our versatile spaces offer room for a variety of activities, while our team makes sure every detail is executed perfectly.

  • Everything from a Single Source

    From planning to execution, we’re at your side. Our experienced team takes care of decor, scheduling, technical setup, and catering so you can sit back and relax. We make sure your winter celebration runs smoothly and full of atmosphere.
