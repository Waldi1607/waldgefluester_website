Funeral Reception (Trauercafé) at Waldgeflüster

Jetzt kontaktieren
↓

At Waldgeflüster, you will find a lovingly designed setting in the heart of nature. In quiet surroundings, embraced by the forest, there is space to come together after the funeral service – with your closest family or with everyone who wishes to say goodbye.

Rooms & Outdoor Area – Exclusively Yours

During your funeral reception, the indoor lounge and outdoor area belong exclusively to you – to family and loved ones. No everyday business, no other guests – simply space and time to be together.

  • Heated indoor space – seating for up to 40 guests
  • Sheltered outdoor area with a forest backdrop
  • Exclusive use – no other guests

Cake, Light Fare & Buffet

Whether coffee and cake, light fare, or a buffet: we arrange the catering according to your personal wishes and calmly coordinate every detail with you in advance. On this day, there is nothing you need to take care of.

A Place for You

  • A place to rest.

    Retreat, take a deep breath, and let the day draw to a gentle close in a sheltered atmosphere.

  • A place to remember.

    Share memories and stories – in whatever way feels right for you and your family.

  • A place just for you.

    Free of time pressure and away from outside eyes: on this day, Waldgeflüster is yours alone.
