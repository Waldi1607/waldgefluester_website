Winter events and Christmas parties at WaldgeflüsterCompany celebrations and Bavarian curling

In winter, Waldgeflüster transforms into a magical backdrop for special occasions. Our venue offers the perfect setting for Christmas parties, winter festivities, and corporate events – enjoy the first snow, cozy fire pits, and warm mulled-wine receptions in the heart of the forest.

Experience the warmth of our fire pit, the pleasure of a welcoming mulled-wine reception, and the fun of a round of Bavarian curling together. Every moment is accompanied by the quiet beauty of the winter forest, turning your celebration into an unforgettable experience.
