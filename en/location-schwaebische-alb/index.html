Your possibilities at our forest event venue

A place full of inspiration

Our forest event venue impresses with its versatility: from festive weddings and creative corporate events to lively birthday parties. Here you can bring your visions to life – whether in an intimate circle or with a large party. The expansive grounds and natural elements create an atmosphere your guests will love.

Indoors and outdoors – space for every occasion

Our generous event grounds span 2,000 m² and include:

  • An expansive clearing for open-air events.
  • A stylish marquee with room for up to 110 guests.
  • A cozy fire pit, perfect for conversations and relaxed moments.
  • Flexible spaces that can be tailored to the needs of your celebration.

Summer or winter – our venue adapts to your needs.

Technical equipment and more

We offer state-of-the-art equipment to make your event perfect:

  • Technology: DJ booth, microphones, speakers, and lighting.
  • Furniture: Elegant tables, crossback chairs, and bench seating.
  • Extras: Mini golf lanes for fun and entertainment.
